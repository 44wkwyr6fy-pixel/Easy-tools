About EasyTools

EasyTools is a free online calculator website designed to make everyday calculations simple and easy.

Our Purpose

We provide simple and useful calculators that anyone can use online without installing an app.

Our Tools

EasyTools currently provides calculators for Bitcoin profit, BMI, percentage, simple interest, compound interest, and GST.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide fast, simple, and easy-to-use online tools for everyone.
